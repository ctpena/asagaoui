The illustrations provided by AsagaoUI are based on "[Illustration and Icon Materials](https://www.digital.go.jp/policies/servicedesign/designsystem/Illustration_Icons)" (Digital Agency). We have performed necessary asset optimization, organized illustrations with the same purpose according to the usage purpose and context, and set appropriate alternative text to enable efficient use of illustrations. In addition, we plan to add our own illustrations in the future while complying with the Digital Agency's guidelines.
